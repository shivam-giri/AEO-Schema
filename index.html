What is Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)?

Answer Engine Optimization (AEO) structures web content using machine-readable JSON-LD schemas and direct question-answer formatting so Large Language Models and AI search engines can parse, trust, and cite your brand accurately.

How does AEO Schema Generation work?

AEO Studio analyzes website DOM structures, page type signals (Homepage, Article, News & Media, Contact Us, Board of Directors, FAQ), and metadata to generate valid, machine-readable JSON-LD schemas tailored for AI indexing.

Why are JSON-LD Schemas important for AI search engines?

JSON-LD schemas provide structured entity definitions for brand identity, corporate governance, press releases, and Q&A pairs. This eliminates hallucination and establishes high E-E-A-T credibility across AI crawlers.
